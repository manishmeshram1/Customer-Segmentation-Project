# Customer Segmentation & Analysis Dashboard

## Project Description

**Customer Segmentation & Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze customer behavior and divide customers into meaningful segments based on their purchasing patterns and characteristics.

The dashboard uses customer attributes such as **Annual Income, Purchase Frequency, Total Spend, Recency, Age, Satisfaction Score, Preferred Category, and Preferred Channel** to identify different customer groups and understand their behavior.

The project helps businesses identify high-value customers, understand purchasing preferences, analyze customer engagement, and make data-driven marketing decisions.

## Key Features

* Customer segmentation using purchasing behavior and customer attributes.
* Analysis of **Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM)** characteristics.
* Interactive customer segment visualization.
* Customer demographic analysis based on age, gender, city, and income.
* Analysis of preferred product categories and purchasing channels.
* Customer satisfaction analysis.
* Interactive filters/slicers for exploring different customer segments.
* KPI cards for quick overview of customer performance.

## Dataset

The project uses a customer dataset containing **600 customer records** and **15 attributes**:

* Customer ID
* Age
* Gender
* City
* Annual Income
* Join Date
* Tenure
* Purchase Frequency
* Average Order Value
* Total Spend
* Last Purchase Date
* Days Since Last Purchase
* Preferred Category
* Preferred Channel
* Satisfaction Score

## Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations
* **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset/source data
* **Power BI Clustering** – Customer segmentation

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard focuses on:

1. **Customer Overview** – Total customers and key customer metrics.
2. **Customer Segmentation** – Identifying groups of customers with similar behavior.
3. **Purchase Behavior** – Understanding frequency, spending, and recency.
4. **Customer Preferences** – Analyzing preferred categories and channels.
5. **Customer Demographics** – Comparing customers by age, gender, city, and income.
6. **Customer Satisfaction** – Understanding satisfaction levels across segments.

## Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to transform raw customer data into actionable insights that can help businesses:

* Identify high-value customers.
* Understand different customer segments.
* Create targeted marketing campaigns.
* Improve customer retention.
* Identify customer purchasing preferences.
* Improve customer experience and satisfaction.
* Support data-driven business decisions.

This project demonstrates how **Power BI, Power Query, DAX, and customer segmentation techniques** can be combined to convert raw customer data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

The final dashboard provides a clear view of customer behavior and helps businesses understand **who their customers are, how they purchase, what they prefer, and which customer segments are most valuable**.
